# Valley Almanac

A pocket companion for Stardew Valley (1.6) that follows your in-game date: today's birthdays and festivals, forgiving search, gift tastes, a Community Center bundle tracker, crops, fish, forage, recipes and how-to guides.

Open it on your phone and use **Share → Add to Home Screen** to get it as an app. Progress is saved in your browser.
